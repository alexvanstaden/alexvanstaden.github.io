Health BoK

Health BoK is a personal, single-user tool that builds a private body of knowledge from health and longevity videos, articles and papers chosen by its owner.

It is self-hosted on a private network and is not offered as a service to the public. There is no sign-up and no public access.

Use of YouTube

The app connects to its owner's own YouTube account for one purpose: removing videos from a private "to process" playlist once they have been reviewed.
