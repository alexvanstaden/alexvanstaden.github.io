Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Health BoK is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.

Google user data

The app requests access to the owner's own YouTube account (the youtube.force-ssl scope) solely to remove items from a playlist the owner created. It does not read, store or process any other YouTube or Google account data.

The OAuth credential is stored only on the owner's self-hosted server. No Google user data is shared with, transferred to or sold to any third party, and none is used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
